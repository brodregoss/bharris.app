# Photo processing

DRAFT for lawyer review. This is not the live terms or privacy policy.

## Terms

Photo import is invitation-only during the beta. A person confirms they are at least 18, that they will only upload their own study materials, and that they will not upload sexually explicit, illegal, or non-consensual content. Content that breaks these rules may be removed and the account terminated.

We do not store the photos. A photo is held in memory on the device and on the server only long enough to check it and read it, then it is discarded. The text extracted from the photo, including titles, terms, and definitions, may be kept with the note.

Each photo is sent to Google Cloud Vision to check it for prohibited content, and to OpenAI to read the notes. Google Cloud Vision and OpenAI may retain those inputs under their own terms. We do not control that retention.

## Privacy

Each photo is sent to two processors: Google Cloud Vision, which checks the photo for prohibited content, and OpenAI, which reads the notes. We do not store the photo. Google Cloud Vision and OpenAI may retain those inputs under their own terms. Deleting an account does not delete a copy a provider has already kept.

Asking OpenAI not to keep a stored completion does not, by itself, turn off a provider's separate abuse-monitoring retention.

Photo access codes are stored as hashes. The app does not save the code.
